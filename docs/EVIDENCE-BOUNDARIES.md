## Runtime and product boundaries

- A **single observation** can support retrieval and interpretation, but cannot establish whether a source changed.
- A **pair with no observed text/confirmation difference** supports that limited no-change finding for those two observations, not a claim of live synchronization.
- A **historical lifecycle replay** can demonstrate correction/withdrawal handling using recorded evidence, but does not show those mutations happening on Bee today.
- The **live v1.1 bridge** demonstrated genuine acquisition plus fresh-process recovery once, not a general task assistant, a learning model, or universal device capture.
- Hardware/source-device origin remains unverified. No private conversations or account metadata were read for documentation alignment.
- Same-development-PC clean extraction does not establish independent physical-machine reproducibility.
- Rights clearance, license adoption and publication authorization remain separate gates from test success.
